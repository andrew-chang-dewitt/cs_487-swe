## instructions

Each student must submit an assessment report with a cover page plus 3 pages covering the following:

1. your team experience including your role on the team,
1. the role of each of your teammates and an assessment of your team's performance,
1. what aspects of your role could have been more automated and how,
1. your assessment of the use of AI tools throughout this project, and
1. how you will apply what you have learned to future project efforts.
